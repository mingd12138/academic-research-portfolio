## Research experience represented

**Natural language processing:** text selection and preprocessing, linguistic analysis, topic modelling (LDA), VADER and TextBlob sentiment analysis, and comparison of machine-learning/deep-learning emotion-classification approaches.

**Quantitative user-experience evaluation:** questionnaire design, reliability assessment, exploratory factor analysis, hierarchical indicator-based evaluation, and interpretation of subjective ratings.

**Research judgement:** awareness of sampling and label-design assumptions, differences between training and target data, and limits on conclusions supported by model outputs.
